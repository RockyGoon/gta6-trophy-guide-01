    Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

    About 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals. The order below is built from the official descriptions: story first, then the side strands, then the free-roam cleanup — with the single missable trophy done in Chapter 1, before anything else. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so never use them on your trophy save. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy in the whole list can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude — Lucia's balcony meditation at Jason's house, available only in Chapter 1 after the mission Down to Earth. It is gone once Chapter 1 ends. Do it before you leave Chapter 1 and keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops. Everything else can be finished in free roam after the credits.

      What counts toward 100%

      The gold trophy A Long Road needs the in-game 100% checklist (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion, visible after the story). Its parts are the story, the side strands, 15 of the 18 meditation spots, the collectible card sets, the challenges and the exploration of all 52 areas. Nothing on that checklist is missable.

      ⚠ 1 MISSABLE TROPHY IN THE WHOLE GAME

      Positive Mental Attitude Bronze

      Official description: “Meditate at every spot across Leonida.”

      In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, find Lucia in the kitchen at Jason's house in the morning, when she talks about meditating. Follow her out to the balcony and accept her invitation. That balcony meditation raises your meditation counter by one, but it is not marked as a normal meditation spot — and whether the trophy strictly needs it is still unconfirmed by the guides. Do it anyway: it costs two minutes, and skipping it risks the whole platinum. Keep a manual save from the start of Chapter 1 until the trophy pops.

      For the other spots: meditation spots show as purple icons when you are close, and meditating at a spot with both Jason and Lucia counts twice — always bring your partner. Track the count under Progress > Collections > Exploration > Meditations. There are 18 spots in total; 15 count toward 100%.
